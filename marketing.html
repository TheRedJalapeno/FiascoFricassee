Brand Marketing

Current Status: Great

Key Initiatives:

  • Increase social media presence: Scale from 1× to 3× daily posts through "Pete the Panther" persona
    • Content focus: Student activities, academic events, awards, capstone projects, sports
    • Prioritize engagement based on platform popularity
  • Establish hashtag campaigns: Create and promote #FLTech hashtags
    • Place promotional placards around campus during events
    • Encourage students to post their own photos and videos
  • Florida Tech Newsletter Blog:
    • Ensure all newsletters remain permanently accessible on the website
3

Performance Marketing

Data-Driven Approach:

  • Dedicated performance marketers for each college
    • Target market specifically to students of each field
    • Business for business students, Engineering for engineers, etc.
  • Performance marketing as distinct from brand marketing
    • Primarily a data analyst role focused on KPI optimization
    • Zero overlap with brand marketing functions
  • Governance process
    • All campaigns require approval from Brand and Legal before launch

Performance marketing focuses on measurable results and conversion metrics, while brand marketing builds awareness and reputation.

4

Student Government Liaison

Driving Optimism on Campus:

  • Open two-way dialogue between:
    • Students
    • Student Government
    • University Leadership
  • Identify issues and publicly resolve them
    • Create forums for student feedback
    • Showcase actions taken based on student input

Strategic Approach

"People like to complain, so let's give them space to action their good ideas"

Transform complaints into constructive improvements and positive stories

5

Public Relations Team

Longer Term Activities:

  • Regular press releases: Minimum twice yearly
    • Faculty publications and research
    • Student showcases and events
    • International programs, study abroad opportunities
  • Medium.com Contributions:
    • Publish newsletter content on major platforms
    • Include links back to fit.edu for traffic generation
  • Research Portal:
    • Blog-style repository of Florida Tech published research
    • Include links to journal articles
  • Google News AMP integration:
    • Optimize posts for ranking on Google News
  • Alumni Evangelism:
    • Champion graduates to enhance public perception
    • Position Florida Tech as an educational leader
6

Corporate Engagement

Building Business Relationships:

Executive Outreach

  • Invite corporate senior leaders (Director+) to student showcases
    • Connect star students with local leaders
    • Position Florida Tech as talent pipeline
  • Engage local CXOs and government leaders
    • Host executive panels and symposiums
    • Build relationships through VIP events
    • Publicly recognize their participation

Benefits

  • Enhanced university reputation
  • Expanded job opportunities for students
  • Potential for increased donor engagement
  • Research partnerships and funding
7

Additional Strategic Initiatives

  • College Ranking Specialist:
    • Reports directly to Dr. Nicklow
    • Provides steering plans for all Deans
    • Target: Improve university ranking to top 100
  • Career Services Transformation:
    • Evolve from traditional model to recruiting agency approach
    • Partner with major brands to fill staffing gaps
    • Generate revenue through placement fees
    • Focus on positions paying $70-150K
  • New Course Development:
    • Research fundamentals for freshmen and MBA students
    • Focus on source credibility, citation tracking, data analysis
  • Future Internship Program (2028+):
    • Mandatory internship requirement for undergraduate graduation
    • Model after RIT's successful program

A phased approach to ensure successful execution and measurable results

Immediate Priorities (0-6 months)

  • Establish hashtag campaigns
  • Launch newsletter blog
  • Begin Student Government liaison activities

Short-Term Goals (6-12 months)

  • Begin corporate executive invitations

Mid-Term Goals (1-2 years)

  • Launch research portal
  • Implement Google News AMP integration
  • Establish Medium.com presence
  • Begin alumni evangelism program

Long-Term Vision (2+ years)

  • Career services transformation
  • New course development
  • Advance toward top 100 ranking

9

Success Metrics

Quantitative Measures

  • Enrollment: Application volume, yield rates, student quality
  • Social Media: Engagement rates, follower growth, hashtag usage
  • Content: Newsletter readership, research portal traffic
  • Press: Media mentions, article placements
  • Ranking: Year-over-year improvement in key rankings

Qualitative Measures

  • Reputation: Stakeholder perception surveys
  • Student Satisfaction: Regular feedback mechanisms
  • Corporate Relations: Executive participation rates
  • Faculty Engagement: Contribution to marketing initiatives
  • Alumni: Testimonials and advocacy measurements

Regular reporting cycles will track progress against these metrics with quarterly reviews and annual comprehensive assessment
